SFO Departure Analytics

SFO Departure Analytics is a web-based project that analyzes flight departure data from the San Francisco International Airport. Users can explore daily departure trends from the past 7 days, view the number of flights departing on selected dates, and identify the busiest and least active travel periods throughout the past week. Built with Python, Flask, Pandas, and Matplotlib, this project transforms raw aviation data into clear and simple visualizations and insights.
